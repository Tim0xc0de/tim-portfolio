**Timeline:** Since August 2026 · **Category:** 💼 Professional

Since August 2026, I have been working as an **Enterprise Integration Specialist** at **SV Informatik GmbH**. My work happens where enterprise systems meet: at the interfaces, services, and data flows that hold a grown system landscape together.

## Focus Areas

**Integration Architecture**

- Connecting and orchestrating distributed systems via an **Enterprise Service Bus (ESB)**
- Design and operation of services within a **service-oriented architecture (SOA)**
- Decoupling systems through clearly defined contracts instead of direct point-to-point connections

**Interfaces & APIs**

- Design and implementation of **REST APIs** for cross-system data flows
- Structured data formats and interface contracts (JSON, XML, OpenAPI/YAML)
- Error handling, monitoring, and traceability of message flows

**Backend Development**

- Implementation of integration and backend logic in the **Java** ecosystem
- Object-oriented design and clean separation of layers
- Back-end web development as the foundation for connected applications

**Ways of Working & Tooling**

- Agile development workflows with **JIRA**
- Version control and code reviews with **Gitea**

## Building on My Background

This work connects directly to my previous years in .NET backend development: there I consolidated isolated utilities into a central microservice architecture and operated REST interfaces for enterprise platforms. The same principles – clear contracts, loose coupling, observable processes – now shape my work on integration solutions, just at the level of the entire system landscape.
